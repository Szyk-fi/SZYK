# SoundFonts

Drop `.sf2` (or `.sf3`) banks in this folder and the **SoundFont** app lists
them. Nothing here is committed: banks are large and each has its own licence,
so they are yours to supply.

Any General MIDI bank works (the app plays its presets on one channel), as do
single-instrument fonts. Plenty of free ones exist; check a bank's licence
before you share a build that includes it.

The device holds a whole bank in memory, so on the real hardware keep an eye on
size: a 30 MB bank is comfortable, a 250 MB one is not.
