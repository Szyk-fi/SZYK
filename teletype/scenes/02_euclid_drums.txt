EUCLID DRUMS
Four euclidean rhythms on TR 1-4 over a 16-step count in X.
Pad 1 changes the fills.
On the grid: the top two faders set the kick and snare fills, the third
the speed; the four buttons under them mute tracks 1-4; the row below is
the 16-step count.
