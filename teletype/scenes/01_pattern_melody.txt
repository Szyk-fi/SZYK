PATTERN MELODY
The metro walks pattern 0 into CV 1 and pulses TR 1.
Pad 1 reverses the pattern, 2 shuffles it, 3 transposes up a fourth.
On the grid: eight faders are the eight steps (press a key to set its
note), the row under them is the playhead, and the bottom row's three
buttons are pads 1-3.
