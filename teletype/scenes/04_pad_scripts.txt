PAD SCRIPTS
Nothing runs on its own: play scripts 1-8 from the SCRIPTS pads.
1-3 and 5 are notes, 4 is an arpeggio, 6 a random bass,
7 changes the note length and 8 starts and stops the metro.
On the grid: the top row of big pads plays scripts 1-4, the row under it
plays the random bass (script 6), and the fader on the bottom row sets the
note length.

#1
CV 1 N 48; TR.P 1

#2
CV 1 N 55; TR.P 1

#3
CV 1 N 60; TR.P 1

#4
$ 1
DEL 120: $ 2
DEL 240: $ 3
DEL 360: $ 5

#5
CV 1 N 64; TR.P 1

#6
CV 2 N RRAND 36 48
TR.P 2

#7
TR.TIME 1 ADD 40 MUL 25 G.FDR.N 0

#8
M.ACT EZ M.ACT

#M
PROB 40: $ 6

#I
M 250; TR.TIME 1 150; TR.TIME 2 400; M.ACT 0
L 1 4: G.BTN I MUL 3 I 0 3 3 0 3 I
G.BTX 8 3 3 3 3 0 3 6 4 1
G.FDR 0 0 7 16 1 0 15 7; G.FDR.N 0 4
