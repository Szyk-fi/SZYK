DRUNK WALK
A melody that wanders a step at a time, and a bass note
every eighth tick. Pad 1 sends the walk back to the middle.
On the grid: the walk is drawn as it goes, one column per tick. The top
fader sets how far it may wander, the button under it is pad 1.
