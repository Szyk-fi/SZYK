# Polyend's instruments, and what Portamax took from them

October 2026 survey of Polyend's line-up (polyend.com and reviews), asking
which ideas fit a phone-sized 4x4-pad machine. Portamax's versions are
original apps: same idea, our own DSP, sequencing and layout. No Polyend
code, presets, sample content or names.

| Polyend | What it is | On Portamax |
|---|---|---|
| **Mess** | Multi-effect pedal: 4 effect slots, each with its own 16-step parameter sequence (lengths, divisions, probability, locks) | **Mosaic** (new): 4 slots in series, 11 effects, per-slot 16-step patterns with length, division, chance and per-step Amount locks. Pads = steps. |
| **Synth** | 3 engine layers, chord modes and "chord followers", arpeggiators | **Trio** (new): 3 Plaits-engine layers; pads play scale degrees or diatonic chords; each layer takes a role (Full / Bass / Top / Arp). |
| **Tracker / Tracker+ / Mini** | Hardware tracker: rows x tracks with note/instrument/volume/FX columns, performance mode | **Ledger** (new): 8 tracks x 64 rows x 16 patterns, note/volume/FX (T M H D R P N), a Plaits voice per track, performance pads (mutes, loops, reverse, octave, half speed, dark). |
| **Press** | Stereo compressor pedal | **Squeeze** (new): soft-knee feed-forward compressor with an external sidechain for ducking. |
| **Loop** | 4 loop tracks + 4 drum tracks, reverse/ping-pong, varispeed | Already covered by **Studio** (8 looping tracks of a minute each) and the drum apps. |
| **Step / Drums / Play / Play+** | Sample drum machines and grooveboxes with chance, play modes, performance FX | Largely covered by **Sequencer**, **Sample Drum**, **Pulsar**; Ledger's performance pads bring the punch-in idea. |
| **Seq** | Standalone MIDI step sequencer | Covered by **Sequencer** and **CV Out**. |
| **Endless** | One user-programmable effect (C++ SDK, text-prompted effects) | Closest today: **Oracle** (AI-designed patches) and **Norns** (scriptable). A scripted-effect slot would be the next step. |
| **Medusa** | Analog/wavetable synth with a 64-pad grid | Hardware-specific; its grid ideas live in the play kit. |
| **Poly / Poly 2, Preset, Anywhere, Keys, Perc Pro** | Eurorack modules, MIDI-to-CV, controllers, a drumming robot | Hardware, not apps. MIDI-to-CV is **CV Out**'s job. |
